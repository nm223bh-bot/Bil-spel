# Project Reflection: Assignment A3

Fill this out together as a group and submit it alongside your final hand-in. Be specific — vague answers ("we used AI a bit") don't give us anything to go on, either for assessing your understanding or for the AI-usage evaluation.

## Group

- **Names:**
- **Game / track:** (Frogger / Doodle Jump / Space Invaders / own idea) — provided game only, provided game + extension, or own idea
- **Grade you're aiming for:** G / VG

## 1. What you built

In 3–5 sentences, describe your game: the core loop, what makes it yours (theme, any extension), and what state it's in at hand-in (fully working / known issues).

## 2. Design reflection

- How did your class design change between your early sketch (week 6/7) and what you actually built? What did you get right, what did you have to rework?
- Where did you use inheritance and polymorphism, and why did you split those classes the way you did rather than some other way?
- If you went for VG: what's your extension, why did you design it that way, and what alternative did you consider and reject?

## 3. Challenges

What was the hardest technical problem you ran into, and how did you solve it? (A bug you chased for hours, a design that didn't work out, a concept that took a while to click — pick whatever was actually hardest, not what sounds impressive.)

## 4. AI usage

Be honest and specific here — this section matters as much as the code itself. For each AI tool you used (name it: GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, etc.):

- **What did you use it for?** (e.g. explaining a concept, generating a first draft of a function, debugging an error message, writing boilerplate, reviewing your code) List concrete examples, not just categories.
- **For your most significant AI-assisted piece of code:** what did you ask for, what did the AI give you, and what did you change, reject, or rewrite yourself — and why?
- **What did you have to understand yourself to use the AI's output correctly?** This is what the weekly talks and the oral component are checking for — if you can't explain a line of code, it shouldn't be in your submission un-reviewed.
- **Is there anything you asked AI for and decided _not_ to use?** Why?
- **Net effect on your learning:** did using AI help you understand OOP/the course material better, worse, or about the same as writing it unassisted would have? Be honest — there's no "correct" answer here.

## 5. What you'd do differently

If you started this project again from scratch, what would you change — about your design, your process, or how you worked together as a pair?
